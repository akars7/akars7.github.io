Ghost Games: what empty stadiums reveal about home advantage

COVID emptied Europe's top five leagues, creating a natural experiment. A regression that controls for team strength shows 60–68% of home advantage disappeared without fans, and the referee's yellow-card lean toward the home side vanished completely. Betting markets adjusted, but not enough.
